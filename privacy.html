Munchkin privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who uses it

Munchkin is used only by its owner. No one else can sign in to it.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads the owner's messages and sends emails the owner has approved. It has no permission to delete or change existing mail.
  • Google Calendar: reads and manages the owner's events.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own requests: answering questions, setting reminders, summarizing, and sending replies the owner approves. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Where it goes

Data is stored on the owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored there too, readable only by the owner's account. When a request needs it, the relevant email or calendar content is sent to the AI model service the owner uses (currently OpenAI, through the owner's ChatGPT account). Answers go back to the owner through Telegram. No person other than the owner reads it.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
